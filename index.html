    Where do you source products?

    Exclusively from licensed dispensaries and authorized suppliers. We do not purchase from grey-market sources.

    How do you verify authenticity?

    We inspect tamper-evident seals, confirm batch/lot info, and cross-check with supplier data. If an item doesn’t pass, it’s re-ordered from the dispensary and checked again. Nothing ships until it’s fully authenticated.

    How fast will my order arrive?

    Orders placed before 2 PM Pacific on a business day are packed the next business day, then delivered by USPS in 2–4 business days with full tracking.

    Is the packaging discreet?

    Yes. Every order ships in a plain outer box with no logos or product names on it.

    What if something’s wrong with my order?

    Every order is covered by our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. Reach out through our contact page and we’ll make it right.

    Do you offer bulk pricing?

    Yes. Our 10 and 20-packs come in at a lower price per unit — see Bulk & Save under Best Sellers. For larger orders, contact us for a quote.
